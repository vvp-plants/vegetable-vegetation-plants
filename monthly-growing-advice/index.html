July Growing Advice

July is warm, bright, and perfect for fast-growing vegetables and flowers. Keep plants well-watered and mulch beds to retain moisture.

  • Water early in the morning
  • Harvest courgettes regularly
  • Pinch out tomato side shoots
  • Deadhead flowers to encourage more blooms

August Growing Advice

August is peak harvest time. Keep feeding fruiting plants and prepare beds for autumn crops.

  • Feed tomatoes and peppers weekly
  • Plant autumn salads
  • Collect seeds from flowers
  • Trim lavender after flowering

September Growing Advice

As temperatures cool, prepare your garden for autumn growth and winter protection.

  • Plant spring bulbs
  • Start winter lettuce
  • Cut back perennials
  • Clear fallen leaves

Top Tip

Water deeply once or twice a week instead of little and often.

Soil Health

Add compost every month to keep soil rich and full of nutrients.

Wildlife Friendly

Leave a small patch of your garden wild to help insects thrive.
