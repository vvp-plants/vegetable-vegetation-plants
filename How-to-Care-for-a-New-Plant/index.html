How to Care for a New Plant

Getting a New Plant

When you bring home a new plant, it is important to give it time to settle into its new environment. Place it somewhere with suitable light and avoid repotting immediately unless it looks overcrowded.

Check the label or plant type to understand its basic needs, such as sunlight, watering, and temperature.

How to Sow Seeds

  • Fill a pot or seed tray with compost.
  • Gently water the compost so it is damp, not soaking wet.
  • Place the seeds on the surface and lightly cover them with compost.
  • Keep the soil moist and place the pot in a warm, bright location.
  • Seeds should start to grow within a few days or weeks.

How to Water Your Plant

Watering your plant correctly is essential for healthy growth. Most plants prefer soil that is moist but not waterlogged.

  • Check the soil before watering – only water if it feels dry.
  • Water slowly so it reaches the roots.
  • Avoid leaving the plant sitting in excess water.
  • Water less often during colder months.

Happy planting! 🌱
