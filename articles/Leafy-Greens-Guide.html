Leafy Greens Guide

Growing Leafy Greens

Leafy greens are fast-growing, nutritious, and perfect for gardeners who want continuous harvests. They thrive in cooler weather, rich soil, and consistent moisture.

A thriving bed of leafy greens: lettuce, kale, spinach, and chard.

Why Leafy Greens Are Great

Leafy greens are some of the easiest and most rewarding crops. They grow quickly, can be harvested repeatedly, and fit into small spaces, containers, or raised beds.

Popular Leafy Greens

These greens are reliable, productive, and ideal for both beginners and experienced gardeners.

Lettuce

Loose-leaf varieties allow continuous harvesting. Crisphead types need cooler weather.

  • Prefers moist soil
  • Grows well in partial shade
  • Harvest outer leaves regularly

Spinach

Spinach thrives in cool temperatures and can bolt quickly in heat.

  • Best in spring and autumn
  • Rich, fertile soil improves yield
  • Keep well-watered

Kale

A hardy, nutrient-rich crop that produces through autumn and winter.

  • Very cold-tolerant
  • Pick leaves from the bottom up
  • Great for smoothies and cooking

Chard

Colourful stems and long cropping season make chard a garden favourite.

  • Heat-tolerant
  • Cut-and-come-again harvests
  • Bright Lights variety is popular

Growing Conditions

Leafy greens thrive when soil, water, and temperature are balanced. These tips help keep plants productive and healthy.

Rich Soil

Add compost regularly. Leafy greens are heavy feeders and respond well to fertile soil.

Consistent Watering

Dry soil leads to bitter leaves. Keep soil evenly moist for tender growth.

Cool Temperatures

Most greens prefer spring and autumn. Provide shade in summer to prevent bolting.

Frequent Harvesting

Harvesting encourages new growth. Pick outer leaves first to keep plants producing.
